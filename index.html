Welcome to Godrej Reserve

Godrej Reserve is a mixed-use development in Kandivali, offering spacious 3 and 4-BHK apartments with balconies and commercial shop spaces. Enjoy a seamless resort-style lifestyle in this prime locale in Mumbai. A grand clubhouse, a 2.5-acre retreat with lush gardens, etc entice you into a world of comfort. The project has commercial shops on the ground and first-floor levels. The commercial elevation presents 45 shops for retail usage. Relish 25+ lifestyle amenities like spring terraces, a gym, a squash court, a swimming pool, an amphitheater, a jogging track, a salon, a cricket field, lush green open spaces, etc. These developments and features enhance the appeal for investors, business owners, and homebuyers.

This coveted mixed-use elevation in Kandivali is an excellent choice due to its connectivity and high visibility. Social infrastructure nearby, including Sharda Gyanpeeth School, ESIS Hospital, Growel’s Mall, Oberoi Mall, Film City, Aarey, etc, are within the proximity of the project. Kandivali Railway Station, Goregaon Metro Station, Aarey Road, Unit No 13 Bus Stop, and the Western Express Highway make commuting easy. The upcoming Akurli metro station will enhance connectivity and reduce traffic. So, revel in the opulent towers at Godrej Reserve in Mumbai’s premium locale.

About Godrej Properties

Founded in 1897, Godrej Properties is a leading real estate developer in India with a range of residential townships, business parks, and mixed-use properties as part of its portfolio. The firm's properties stand out for their lovely architecture, quality residences, tranquil lush environs, and use of cutting-edge technologies. With a grand legacy of 125 years in the real estate industry, Godrej Properties has changed the skyline of many cities in the country.
